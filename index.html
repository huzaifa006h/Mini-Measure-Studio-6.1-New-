06 Detection controls

How it works: the detector now estimates the border/background brightness and searches for either darker or brighter connected foreground regions, then ranks candidates by size, solidity and position. It is still classic image processing—not a trained AI model—and outputs a candidate bounding box, not a guaranteed exact contour. Plain backgrounds and even lighting help most.
Engineering note: perspective distortion and calibration placement can dominate the measurement error.
